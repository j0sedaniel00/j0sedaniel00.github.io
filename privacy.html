Privacy Policy

Ahora Lo Sabes Publisher · Last updated: November 25, 2026

Data we access

When you connect TikTok, the tool receives: an access token, and your basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar) through the user.info.basic scope. It uses the video.upload and video.publish scopes only to post videos you choose.

How it is stored

Tokens are stored only on the creator's own computer, in a file readable only by that computer's user. Nothing is sent to any server other than TikTok's official API. We do not sell, share or use your data for advertising or analytics.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept only while the account is connected. You can revoke access at any time in TikTok (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions); the local token file can be deleted on request.

Children

The tool is not directed at children under 13.
