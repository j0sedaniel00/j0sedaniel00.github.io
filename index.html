Ahora Lo Sabes Publisher

Ahora Lo Sabes is a Spanish-language educational short-video channel ("Now you know"): everyday tips about food safety, money, phones and scams, each video 30–45 seconds long.

Ahora Lo Sabes Publisher is the small desktop tool the channel's creator uses to upload finished videos to the creator's own TikTok account through TikTok's official Content Posting API. The creator logs in with TikTok, reviews each video, writes the caption, chooses the privacy level and confirms before anything is posted.

What it does

  • Logs in with TikTok (Login Kit) to read basic profile info (display name, avatar) so the creator can see which account is connected.
  • Uploads a video file chosen by the creator and posts it with the caption and privacy setting the creator selects.
  • Does not read, collect or store data about other TikTok users.
